Online Privacy

Learn how to keep your personal data safe and private.

Why Online Privacy Matters

Your personal information is valuable. From your location to your browsing habits, advertisers and malicious actors try to collect your data without your consent.

Practicing good digital hygiene helps ensure your identity remains protected online.

Top Privacy Practices

  • Check app permissions before granting access to your camera, microphone, or location.
  • Use privacy-focused browsers and search engines.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive details on public social media profiles.
