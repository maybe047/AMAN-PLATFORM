Blackmail Awareness

Know how to respond and protect yourself from digital extortion.

What to Do If You Face Blackmail

Digital blackmail can be frightening, but staying calm and taking the right steps is your best defense. Never give in to demands or pay money.

Extortionists rarely stop after receiving payment; instead, they often demand more.

Immediate Action Steps

  • Do not pay any money or comply with their demands under any circumstances.
  • Save all evidence by taking screenshots of chats, profiles, and links.
  • Block the person immediately across all platforms without further communication.
  • Inform a trusted adult, family member, or specialized cyber support authorities right away.
