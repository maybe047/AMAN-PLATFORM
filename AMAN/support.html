Phishing Awareness

Learn how to spot and avoid digital scams and fraudulent messages.

What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a deceptive tactic where cybercriminals pose as trusted entities (like banks, streaming services, or delivery companies) to steal your sensitive data, passwords, or financial details.

Recognizing warning signs is your primary defense against falling victim to fraudulent links.

Common Signs of Phishing

  • Urgent or threatening language pushing you to act immediately (e.g., "Account suspended!").
  • Misspelled domain names or suspicious URLs that don't match official websites.
  • Unexpected attachments or unexpected requests for personal login details and OTPs.
