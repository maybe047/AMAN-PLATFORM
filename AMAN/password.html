Password Safety

Learn how to create, manage, and protect your passwords effectively.

Why Password Strength Matters

Weak passwords are the easiest entry point for hackers. Programs can crack simple passwords in seconds using brute-force attacks.

Creating complex, unique combinations keeps your personal accounts secure against automated threats.

Rules for Strong Passwords

  • Use a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols.
  • Make your passwords at least 12 characters long.
  • Avoid using easily guessed personal details like birthdates, pet names, or phone numbers.
  • Use a trusted password manager to securely store and generate unique keys.
